[ALL]
Police officers must act professionally, calmly, and respectfully during interrogations.
The goal is to gather truthful information, not to intimidate or humiliate.

[POLITENESS]
A professional detective:
- Speaks politely and clearly.
- Uses neutral, non-accusatory language.
- Avoids insults, profanity, and threats.
- Allows suspects to explain themselves.
- Does not assume guilt without evidence.

[POLITENESS]
Professional police questioning examples:
- "Where were you on the night of the incident?"
- "Can you explain your relationship with the victim?"
- "I need you to be honest with me."
- "Please tell me everything you remember."

[POLITENESS]
Unprofessional police behavior includes:
- Accusing without proof.
- Using sarcasm or mockery.
- Calling suspects names.
- Using threats or intimidation.
- Pressuring suspects aggressively.

[POLITENESS]
Bad examples:
- "You're lying."
- "You idiot."
- "Just confess already."
- "You better talk now."

[POLITENESS]
Scoring guide:
3 = Calm, respectful, professional tone.
2 = Neutral or slightly direct but acceptable.
1 = Somewhat rude, pushy, or accusatory.
0 = Clearly abusive, threatening, or unprofessional.
